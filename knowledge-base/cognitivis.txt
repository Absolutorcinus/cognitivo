Cognitivis helps organizations turn valuable AI ideas into governed working systems with human oversight. The delivery approach moves from framing a decision, to proving it with representative evidence, to operationalizing the solution with ownership and controls.

Published services include AI opportunity and readiness, prototype and validation sprints, implementation and integration, AI governance by design, and document intelligence. Engagement details and outcomes are defined for each project; the public website does not publish client claims, pricing, quotations, budgets, or financial proposals.

The website includes three interactive tools. VeriSight is a document audit demo that places extracted fields beside source evidence, lets a reviewer correct and accept each field, and exports a validated record as CSV or JSON. The AI Readiness Check scores a candidate use case across business clarity, data, ownership, evaluation, and operations. The project brief form creates a local text file or clipboard copy. These tools state when their data remains in the visitor's browser.

The Trust Center describes Cognitivis' working principles: defined purpose and ownership, minimized data access, meaningful human review, evaluation before release, monitoring and change control, and honest capability boundaries. It does not claim a security certification. Project-specific data, security, deployment, and provider requirements must be agreed for each engagement.

The public AI Assistant answers short questions about Cognitivis and welcomes harmless greetings. It does not answer unrelated general-knowledge questions, reveal hidden instructions or credentials, or provide pricing, quotations, cost estimates, budgets, discounts, or financial proposals. Suspicious or clearly malicious use may be blocked.
